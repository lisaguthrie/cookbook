Egg Salad & Spinach Sandwich with Grape Tomatoes

Serves 6 · Cook 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ pkgs baby spinach
  • 6 sticks celery
  • 600g (12) eggs
  • 9g (3 cloves) garlic
  • 1.1kg (3 pints) grape tomatoes
  • 1 ½ small bunches green onions (scallions)
  • 12 slices whole grain bread
  • 1g (⅜ tsp) black pepper
  • 15ml (3 tsp) Dijon mustard
  • 0.5g (¾ tsp) dill, dried
  • 133ml (9 tbsp) mayonnaise
  • 1g (⅜ tsp) paprika
  • 4.5g (¾ tsp) salt

Instructions

  1. Place eggs in a small saucepan, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for 8 minutes. Drain hot water and add ice or run under cold water to cool.

    600g (12) eggs

  2. Peel and mince garlic. In a large bowl, prepared dressing by mixing together garlic, mayo, Dijon, dill, paprika, salt, and pepper.

    9g (3 cloves) garlic · 133ml (9 tbsp) mayonnaise · 15ml (3 tsp) Dijon mustard · 0.5g (¾ tsp) dill · 1g (⅜ tsp) paprika · 4.5g (¾ tsp) salt · 1g (⅜ tsp) black pepper

  3. Wash celery and green onions. Small dice celery and add to bowl with dressing. Trim and discard green onion roots ends, chop, and add to bowl.

    6 sticks celery · 1 ½ small bunch green onions (scallions)

  4. Wash and dry spinach.

    1 ½ pkg baby spinach

  5. Wash tomatoes.

    1.1kg (3 pints) grape tomatoes

  6. Toast bread.

    12 slices whole grain bread

  7. Peel, medium dice, and add eggs to bowl with other ingredients. Stir to combine.
  8. Place bread in pairs on a flat surface. Arrange spinach leaves over one slice of bread in each pair, then top with egg salad and a second slice of bread.
  9. Serve sandwiches with tomatoes on the side and enjoy!
