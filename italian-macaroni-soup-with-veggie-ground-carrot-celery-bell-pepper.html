Italian Macaroni Soup with Veggie Ground, Carrot, Celery & Bell Pepper

Serves 4 · Cook 35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 medium carrots
  • 2 sticks celery
  • 1.4L (6 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  • 246g (2) cans diced tomatoes
  • 12g (4 cloves) garlic
  • 1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 170g (1 ⅔ cup) macaroni (elbow) pasta
  • 238g (2) red bell peppers
  • 454g (2 ¾ cup) veggie ground, plant based
  • 1 medium yellow onion
  • 30ml (2 tbsp) balsamic vinegar
  • 1.2g (½ tsp) black pepper
  • 39ml (2 ⅔ tbsp) extra virgin olive oil, divided
  • 1.7g (2 tsp) Italian seasoning
  • 6g (1 tsp) salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  2. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat. Add the veggie ground and spices; cook, breaking it apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 4-5 minutes. Transfer veggie ground to a plate and set aside. (Reserve pot for later use.)

    20ml (4 tsp) extra virgin olive oil · 454g (2 ¾ cup) veggie ground, plant based · 1.7g (2 tsp) Italian seasoning · 6g (1 tsp) salt · 1.2g (½ tsp) black pepper

  3. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.

    238g (2) red bell peppers · 2 medium carrots · 2 sticks celery · 1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley

  4. Trim, seed, and small dice bell peppers. Place in a medium bowl.
  5. Peel, trim, and small dice carrots. Add to the bowl with the bell pepper.
  6. Trim and small dice celery. Add to the bowl.
  7. Peel, halve, and medium dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Add both to the bowl.

    1 medium yellow onion · 12g (4 cloves) garlic

  8. Return the pot to medium-high heat.
  9. Once the pot is hot, add more oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the veggies and cook, stirring occasionally, until starting to soften, 3-4 minutes.

    20ml (4 tsp) extra virgin olive oil

  10. Return veggie ground to the pot along with broth, tomatoes, pasta, and vinegar. Stir to combine the soup, cover, and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat to medium and simmer until pasta is cooked through, about 8 minutes.

    1.4L (6 cup) chicken or vegetable broth · 246g (2) cans diced tomatoes · 170g (1 ⅔ cup) macaroni (elbow) pasta · 30ml (2 tbsp) balsamic vinegar

  11. Meanwhile, shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves.
  12. Once the pasta is done, add half of the parsley to the soup and stir to combine. Remove from heat. (Reserve remaining parsley for serving.)
  13. To serve, divide soup between bowls and sprinkle with reserved parsley. Enjoy!
