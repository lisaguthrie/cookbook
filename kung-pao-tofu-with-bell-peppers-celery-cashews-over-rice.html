"Kung Pao" Tofu with Bell Peppers, Celery & Cashews over Rice

Serves 6 · Cook 40 min

Ingredients

  • 102.8g (¾ cup) cashews, roasted unsalted
  • 3 sticks celery
  • 3 pkgs extra firm tofu
  • 9g (3 cloves) garlic
  • 3 pieces ginger root
  • 1 small bunch green onions (scallions)
  • 277.5g (1 ½ cups) jasmine rice
  • 357g (3) orange bell peppers
  • 357g (3) red bell peppers
  • 41.3g (3 tbsp) brown sugar
  • 22ml (1 ½ tbsp) chili-garlic sauce
  • 47g (⅜ cup) cornstarch, divided
  • 44ml (3 tbsp) rice vinegar
  • 7.5g (1 ¼ tsp) salt, divided
  • 79.8ml (⅓ cup) soy sauce
  • 89ml (6 tbsp) toasted sesame oil, divided
  • 887ml (3 ¾ cup) water, divided

Instructions

  1. Cut tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place on a baking sheet pan. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.

    3 pkg extra firm tofu

  2. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add water and salt, then bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.

    277.5g (1 ½ cups) jasmine rice · 710ml (3 cup) water · 3g (½ tsp) salt

  3. Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce heat to low. Cook rice until liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  4. Medium dice tofu. Place in a large bowl, sprinkle with cornstarch and salt, then gently toss to coat.

    43g (⅓ cup) cornstarch · 4.5g (¾ tsp) salt

  5. Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  6. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Working in batches if necessary, add the tofu and cook, turning every few minutes, until golden brown and crispy, about 10 minutes; remove to a plate. (Reserve bowl for later use.)

    44ml (3 tbsp) toasted sesame oil

  7. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.

    357g (3) red bell peppers · 357g (3) orange bell peppers · 3 sticks celery · 3 pieces ginger root · 1 small bunch green onions (scallions)

  8. Trim, seed, and large dice bell peppers. Trim and large dice celery. Place both in the reserved bowl and set aside.
  9. Peel and mince ginger and garlic. Place both in a medium bowl.

    9g (3 cloves) garlic

  10. Trim and cut green onions crosswise into 1-inch pieces. Add to the bowl with the ginger and garlic.
  11. When the tofu is done, return skillet to medium-high heat, add more oil, and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the bell pepper and celery; cook, stirring occasionally, until tender-crisp, 5-6 minutes.

    30ml (2 tbsp) toasted sesame oil

  12. While the veggies are cooking, add water, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, chili-garlic sauce, additional cornstarch, and oil to the small bowl. Whisk to combine the sauce.

    177ml (¾ cup) water · 79.8ml (⅓ cup) soy sauce · 44ml (3 tbsp) rice vinegar · 41.3g (3 tbsp) brown sugar · 22ml (1 ½ tbsp) chili-garlic sauce · 12g (1 ½ tbsp) cornstarch · 15ml (1 tbsp) toasted sesame oil

  13. When the veggies are done, add crispy tofu, sauce, and cashews to the skillet. Bring the sauce to a boil and cook, stirring frequently, until thickened, about 2 minutes. Remove from heat.

    102.8g (¾ cup) cashews, roasted unsalted

  14. To serve, divide rice and stir-fry between bowls. Enjoy!
