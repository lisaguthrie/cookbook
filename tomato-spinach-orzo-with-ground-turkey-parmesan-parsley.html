Tomato-Spinach Orzo with Ground Turkey, Parmesan & Parsley

Serves 6 · Cook 35 min

Ingredients

  • 3 pkgs baby spinach
  • 710ml (3 cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1kg (2 ¼ lb) ground turkey
  • 1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley
  • 425g (4 ¼ cup) orzo pasta
  • 85g (¾ cup) Parmesan cheese
  • ½ medium red onion
  • 3 cans tomato sauce
  • 1.8g (¾ tsp) black pepper
  • 4ml (¾ tsp) crushed red pepper
  • 30ml (2 tbsp) extra virgin olive oil
  • 4.7g (1 ½ tsp) garlic powder
  • 2.5g (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning
  • 9g (1 ½ tsp) salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat a large pot over medium-high heat.
  2. While the pot heats up, peel, halve, and mince onion.

    ½ medium red onion

  3. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion, turkey, and spices; cook, breaking the meat apart with a spoon, until browned and crumbly, 5-6 minutes.

    30ml (2 tbsp) extra virgin olive oil · 1kg (2 ¼ lb) ground turkey · 2.5g (1 tbsp) Italian seasoning · 4.7g (1 ½ tsp) garlic powder · 9g (1 ½ tsp) salt · 1.8g (¾ tsp) black pepper · 4ml (¾ tsp) crushed red pepper

  4. Add broth, tomato sauce, and orzo to the pot; stir to combine and bring to a simmer. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and cook, stirring occasionally, until liquid is mostly absorbed and orzo is desired firmness, 10-15 minutes.

    710ml (3 cup) chicken or vegetable broth · 3 cans tomato sauce · 425g (4 ¼ cup) orzo pasta

  5. Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan. Place in a small bowl.

    85g (¾ cup) Parmesan cheese

  6. Wash, dry, and shave parsley leaves off the stems; discard stems and mince the leaves. Add to the bowl with the Parmesan, stir to combine, and set aside.

    1 small bunch Italian (flat-leaf) parsley

  7. Wash and dry spinach (skip this step if it came pre-washed).

    3 pkgs baby spinach

  8. When the orzo is done, add spinach to the pot in handfuls, waiting for it to wilt slightly before adding the next handful.
  9. Add half of the Parmesan-parsley mixture to the pot and stir to combine. Remove from heat. (Reserve remaining Parmesan mixture for serving.)
  10. To serve, divide orzo between plates or bowls; top with reserved Parmesan mixture. Enjoy!
