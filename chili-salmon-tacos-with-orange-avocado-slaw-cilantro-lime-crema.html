Chili Salmon Tacos with Orange-Avocado Slaw, Cilantro & Lime Crema

Serves 4 · Cook 35 min

Ingredients

  • 150g (1) avocado
  • 1 pkg broccoli slaw
  • 1 small bunch cilantro
  • 1 jalapeño pepper
  • 88g (2) limes
  • 524g (4) oranges
  • 122.5g (½ cup) plain Greek yogurt
  • 680g (1 ½ lb) salmon fillet
  • 80g (2) shallots
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1.2g (½ tsp) black pepper
  • 8.1g (1 tbsp) chili powder
  • 3.1g (1 ½ tsp) cumin, ground
  • 25ml (1 ⅔ tbsp) extra virgin olive oil, divided
  • 7.5g (1 ¼ tsp) salt, divided

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet pan with aluminum foil.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.

    88g (2) limes · 1 jalapeño pepper · 524g (4) oranges · 1 small bunch cilantro · 150g (1) avocado

  3. Juice one lime into a small bowl. Add yogurt and salt; stir to combine the crema and set aside. (Reserve remaining lime for later use.)

    122.5g (½ cup) plain Greek yogurt · 1.5g (¼ tsp) salt

  4. Place oil and spices in medium bowl, then stir to combine the spice oil.

    15ml (1 tbsp) extra virgin olive oil · 8.1g (1 tbsp) chili powder · 3.1g (1 ½ tsp) cumin, ground · 3g (½ tsp) salt · 1.2g (½ tsp) black pepper

  5. Pat salmon dry with paper towels. Cut into 1-inch thick fingers and add to the bowl with the spice oil. Stir to coat, then transfer to the baking sheet.

    680g (1 ½ lb) salmon fillet

  6. Place baking sheet in the oven (it doesn't have to be fully heated) and bake until salmon is cooked through, about 8 minutes. Remove from oven.
  7. Meanwhile, peel and mince shallots. Place in a clean medium bowl.

    80g (2) shallots

  8. Juice reserved lime into the bowl with the shallot, then add oil and salt. Stir to combine the dressing.

    10ml (2 tsp) extra virgin olive oil · 3g (½ tsp) salt

  9. Quarter jalapeño pepper lengthwise; seed and remove ribs with a spoon. Mince and add to the bowl with the dressing. (Be careful with jalapeños; do not touch your eyes and ensure you wash your hands after handling or wear gloves while preparing.)
  10. Peel oranges, separate into segments, and roughly chop the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  11. Shave cilantro leaves off the stems; discard stems and roughly chop the leaves. Add to the bowl.
  12. Halve and pit avocado; using a spoon, scoop out the flesh and medium dice. Add to the bowl, along with the broccoli slaw, and gently stir to combine.

    1 pkg broccoli slaw

  13. Warm tortillas in a skillet, oven, or microwave (optional).

    12 small corn tortillas

  14. Divide tortillas between plates, then fill with salmon and a small portion of slaw; drizzle with lime crema. Serve with remaining slaw on the side and enjoy!
