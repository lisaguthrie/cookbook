Green goddess chicken salad lettuce cups

Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 8 oz. Chicken breast
  • 1 oz. Red onion
  • 1 oz. Chopped almonds
  • 1 oz. Romain lettuce
  • 150g (1) Avocado
  • 1 oz. Green goddess dressing

Instructions

  1. Salt and pepper chicken and cook in avocado oil
  2. Chop up chicken
  3. Coarsely chop almonds
  4. Slice avocado
  5. Mix chicken, avocado, onion, almonds and dressing in bowl and place on Romain leaves
  6. Serve
