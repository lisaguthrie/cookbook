Potato Mushroom Soup

Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 340g (¾ lb) mushrooms (sliced)
  • 6 medium golden potatoes (rinsed & cut into 1 inch pieces)
  • 122g (2) carrots (-diced)
  • 1 stick celery (-chopped)
  • 75g (½) chopped onion (or 1/2 cup sliced leeks)
  • 12g (4 cloves) garlic (-minced)
  • 710ml (3 cups) water
  • 237ml (1 cup) coconut or cashew milk (full fat)
  • 3g (1 tsp) turmeric
  • 7ml (1 ½ tsp) ground mustard
  • 2 leaves bay
  • 6 sprigs thyme
  • 1 pinch sea salt (-to taste)
  • 1 pinch nutmeg (-freshly grated)
  • 182g (2 cups) baby spinach (or broccoli florets)

Instructions

  1. Preheat a large soup pot over medium flame. Add the onion (or leeks) together with a splash of water and saute until translucent, about 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in the carrots and celery and a pinch of sea salt and cook another 5 minutes or so until softened.
  3. Add the garlic, bay leaf, thyme, ground mustard and turmeric and give everything a quick stir. Add the mushrooms and cook another 5 minutes or so until wilted. Stir in the potatoes and pour in the water.
  4. Bring the soup to a simmer, add a good pinch of sea salt and cover with a lid. Cook for about 20 minutes or so until the potatoes are cooked through.
  5. Adjust seasonings to taste with more sea salt then stir in the spinach. Cook just another minute or so until wilted. (If adding broccoli instead you’ll need to cook it a few more minutes)
  6. Stir in the coconut milk and remove from heat. Serve hot with a sprinkling of nutmeg and freshly cracked black pepper on top.
