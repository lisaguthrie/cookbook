Chicken Kebabs with Greek Salad & Tzatziki Sauce

Serves 6 · Cook 45 min

Ingredients

  • 680g (1 ½ lb) chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 1 ½ pkgs crumbled feta cheese
  • 1 ½ English cucumbers
  • 27g (9 cloves) garlic
  • 540g (1 ½ pints) grape tomatoes
  • 177ml (¾ cup) Kalamata olives, pitted
  • 87g (1 ½) lemons
  • 367.5g (1 ½ cups) plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 ½ heads romaine lettuce
  • 5.4g (2 ¼ tsp) black pepper, divided
  • 133ml (9 tbsp) extra virgin olive oil
  • 1.5g (1 ½ tsp) oregano, dried
  • 44ml (3 tbsp) red wine vinegar
  • 13.5g (2 ¼ tsp) salt, divided

Instructions

  1. Peel and mince the garlic.

    18g (6 cloves) garlic

  2. In a small bowl, whisk together the minced garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, vinegar, oregano, salt, and pepper.

    juice of ¾ lemon · 133ml (9 tbsp) extra virgin olive oil · 44ml (3 tbsp) red wine vinegar · 1.5g (1 ½ tsp) oregano · 9g (1 ½ tsp) salt · 3.6g (1 ½ tsp) black pepper

  3. Cube the chicken and transfer to a medium bowl. Pour half of the dressing over top (save the rest for the salad) and toss to coat.

    680g (1 ½ lb) chicken breasts, boneless skinless

  4. Heat a grill pan, outdoor grill, or skillet over medium-high heat.
  5. Thread the chicken onto 6 skewers. Alternatively, you can cook the chicken pieces directly on the grill/skillet without using skewers.
  6. Add the chicken to the grill or skillet and cook, turning occasionally, until cooked through, 8-10 minutes.
  7. Wash and dry the fresh produce.

    1 ½ head romaine lettuce · 1 ½ English cucumber · 540g (1 ½ pint) grape tomatoes

  8. Finely dice ¼ of the cucumber. Peel and mince the remaining garlic. In a small bowl, prepare the tzatziki sauce by mixing together the cucumber, garlic, lemon juice, yogurt, and salt.

    9g (3 cloves) garlic · juice of ¾ lemon · 367.5g (1 ½ cup) plain Greek yogurt · 4.5g (¾ tsp) salt · 1.8g (¾ tsp) black pepper

  9. Halve the lettuce lengthwise, then chop crosswise into 1 inch strips; transfer to a salad bowl. Quarter the remaining cucumbers lengthwise, then cut crosswise into chunks; add to the bowl. Halve the tomatoes; add to the bowl.
  10. Add the olives, feta, and reserved dressing to the salad and toss.

    177ml (¾ cup) Kalamata olives, pitted · 1 ½ pkg crumbled feta cheese

  11. To serve, place salad on a plate and add a chicken skewer on top. Serve with tzatziki sauce and enjoy!
