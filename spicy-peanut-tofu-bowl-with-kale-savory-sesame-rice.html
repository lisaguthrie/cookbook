Spicy Peanut Tofu Bowl with Kale & Savory Sesame Rice

Serves 4 · Cook 35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 medium carrots
  • 2 pkg extra firm tofu
  • 6g (2 cloves) garlic
  • 2 pieces ginger root
  • 185g (1 cup) jasmine rice
  • 2 bunches kale
  • 15ml (1 tbsp) chili-garlic sauce
  • 64.5g (¼ cup) natural peanut butter
  • 39ml (2 ⅔ tbsp) pure maple syrup, divided
  • 18g (2 tbsp) sesame seeds
  • 69ml (4 ⅔ tbsp) soy sauce, divided
  • 74ml (5 tbsp) toasted sesame oil, divided
  • 640.2ml (2 ⅔ cup) water, divided

Instructions

  1. Cut the tofu in half along the long side. Sandwich the tofu between clean towels or paper towels and place side by side on a baking sheet. Place a cutting board with something heavy on top and set aside to press out excess water.

    2 pkg extra firm tofu

  2. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a small saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.

    185g (1 cup) jasmine rice · 473ml (2 cup) water

  3. Peel and mince the garlic. In a medium bowl, combine garlic, sesame oil, soy sauce, and maple syrup to make a marinade for the tofu.

    6g (2 cloves) garlic · 30ml (2 tbsp) toasted sesame oil · 59ml (¼ cup) soy sauce · 30ml (2 tbsp) pure maple syrup

  4. Once the liquid with the rice comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove the rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, cut the tofu planks in half and then cut each half into 4 triangles. Place tofu in the bowl with the marinade and gently toss until well coated. Set aside.
  6. Wash and dry kale. Fold leaves in half lengthwise and slice off the stems. Chop or tear leaves into bite-sized pieces and transfer to a salad bowl.

    2 bunches kale

  7. Wash, trim, and peel the carrots; coarsely grate and set aside.

    2 medium carrots

  8. Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat.
  9. Wash, peel, and mince or grate the ginger.

    2 pieces ginger root

  10. Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add the tofu and the marinade. Cook until golden brown, 2-3 minutes per side.

    30ml (2 tbsp) toasted sesame oil

  11. Meanwhile, prepare the peanut sauce by whisking together ginger, peanut butter, chili-garlic sauce, soy sauce, maple syrup, and hot water in a small bowl.

    64.5g (¼ cup) natural peanut butter · 15ml (1 tbsp) chili-garlic sauce · 10ml (2 tsp) soy sauce · 10ml (2 tsp) pure maple syrup · 44ml (3 tbsp) water

  12. Once the tofu is cooked, transfer to a plate, returning the skillet to the heat. Add kale and water to the skillet and cover with a lid. Allow to steam for 1 minute covered then uncover, stirring occasionally, for 1 minute more.

    118ml (½ cup) water

  13. Uncover the rice, fluff with a fork, and add sesame oil. Fold the oil into the rice.

    15ml (1 tbsp) toasted sesame oil

  14. To serve, divide rice between bowls, top with tofu, grated carrot, and kale; drizzle with peanut sauce and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Enjoy!

    18g (2 tbsp) sesame seeds
