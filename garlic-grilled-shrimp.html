Garlic Grilled Shrimp

Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 454g (1 pound) shrimp
  • lemon wedges for serving optional
  • 4 cloves garlic minced
  • 5ml (1 teaspoon) parsley chopped
  • 56.8g (¼ cup) butter melted
  • 1 clove garlic minced
  • 2.4g (½ teaspoon) black pepperGarlic Butter
  • 3g (½ teaspoon) salt
  • 15ml (1 tablespoon) tomato paste
  • 10ml (2 teaspoons) lemon juice
  • 15ml (1 tablespoon) fresh basil chopped
  • 3g (2 tablespoons) chopped parsley
  • 79ml (⅓ cup) olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic minced

Instructions

  1. Preheat the grill to medium heat.
