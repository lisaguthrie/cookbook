Sweet & Sour Coconut Popcorn Chicken with Ginger-Garlic Green Beans

Serves 6 · Cook 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1kg (2 ¼ lb) chicken breasts, boneless skinless
  • 150g (3) eggs
  • 18g (6 cloves) garlic
  • 3 pieces ginger root
  • 680g (1 ½ lb) green beans
  • 197g (1 ½) oranges
  • 1 ½ cups shredded unsweetened coconut
  • 93.8g (¾ cup) almond meal/flour
  • 0.6g (¼ tsp) black pepper
  • 20.6g (1 ½ tbsp) brown sugar
  • 8g (1 tbsp) cornstarch
  • 67ml (4 ½ tbsp) extra virgin olive oil, divided
  • 44ml (3 tbsp) ketchup
  • 22ml (1 ½ tbsp) rice vinegar
  • 9g (1 ½ tsp) salt
  • 2.3g (¾ tsp) sesame seeds
  • 22ml (1 ½ tbsp) soy sauce
  • 44ml (3 tbsp) water

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line two baking sheet pans with aluminum foil.
  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.

    680g (1 ½ lb) green beans · 3 pieces ginger root · 197g (1 ½) oranges

  3. Trim and discard ends of the green beans; transfer to one of the baking sheets, in a single layer.
  4. Peel and mince garlic and ginger; sprinkle both over the green beans. Set aside.

    18g (6 cloves) garlic

  5. Place coconut and almond flour in a large bowl; whisk to combine.

    1 ½ cups shredded unsweetened coconut · 93.8g (¾ cup) almond meal/flour

  6. Whisk the eggs in a second large bowl.

    150g (3) eggs

  7. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces; season with salt and pepper.

    1kg (2 ¼ lb) chicken breasts, boneless skinless · 9g (1 ½ tsp) salt · 0.6g (¼ tsp) black pepper

  8. Toss chicken in the egg to coat. Working in batches, transfer chicken to the coconut mixture and turn to coat, pressing gently to adhere. Place chicken on the other the baking sheet, in a single layer.
  9. Drizzle oil over the beans and chicken. Place both baking sheets in the oven and bake until chicken is golden brown and cooked through, and beans are fork-tender, 18-20 minutes.

    44ml (3 tbsp) extra virgin olive oil

  10. Meanwhile, juice oranges into a medium saucepan. Add ketchup, oil, soy sauce, vinegar, and sugar; whisk to combine. Bring to a simmer over medium heat.

    44ml (3 tbsp) ketchup · 22ml (1 ½ tbsp) extra virgin olive oil · 22ml (1 ½ tbsp) soy sauce · 22ml (1 ½ tbsp) rice vinegar · 20.6g (1 ½ tbsp) brown sugar

  11. In a small bowl, make a slurry by whisking water with cornstarch until smooth.

    44ml (3 tbsp) water · 8g (1 tbsp) cornstarch

  12. Add cornstarch slurry to the saucepan in a steady stream while whisking quickly. Cook, whisking often, until sweet and sour sauce thickens, 2-3 minutes. Remove from heat.
  13. Drizzle sweet and sour sauce over the baked chicken and gently toss to coat.
  14. To serve, divide chicken and beans between plates. Sprinkle chicken with sesame seeds and enjoy!

    2.3g (¾ tsp) sesame seeds
