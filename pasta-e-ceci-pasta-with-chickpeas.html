Pasta e Ceci (Pasta with Chickpeas)

Serves 6 · Cook 35 min

Ingredients

  • 3 sticks celery
  • 1.1L (4 ⅔ cup) chicken or vegetable broth
  • 369g (3) cans diced tomatoes
  • 1 ½ small pkgs fresh rosemary
  • 3 cans garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
  • 18g (6 cloves) garlic
  • 510g (5 ⅛ cup) penne pasta
  • 1 ½ medium yellow onions
  • 5.4g (2 ¼ tsp) black pepper
  • 2.3ml (⅜ tsp) crushed red pepper
  • 44ml (3 tbsp) extra virgin olive oil
  • 13.5g (2 ¼ tsp) salt

Instructions

  1. Wash and dry the fresh produce.

    3 sticks celery · 1 ½ small pkg fresh rosemary

  2. Small dice the celery and transfer to a medium bowl.
  3. Preheat a sauté pan over medium heat.
  4. Peel and small dice the onion; add to the bowl with the celery.

    1 ½ medium yellow onion

  5. Add olive oil to the pan and swirl to coat the bottom.

    44ml (3 tbsp) extra virgin olive oil

  6. Add the celery and onion to the pan; cook, stirring frequently, until the vegetables have softened, 4 to 5 minutes.
  7. Peel and mince the garlic; transfer to a small bowl.

    18g (6 cloves) garlic

  8. Slide the rosemary leaves off the sprigs; discard the sprigs and mince the leaves. Add to the garlic.
  9. Add the garlic, rosemary, and crushed red pepper to the pan; stir and cook until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes.

    2.3ml (⅜ tsp) crushed red pepper

  10. Add the chickpeas and their liquid to the pan, followed by the tomatoes and broth; stir to combine.

    3 cans garbanzo beans (chickpeas) · 369g (3) cans diced tomatoes · 1.1L (4 ⅔ cup) chicken or vegetable broth

  11. Season the sauce with salt and pepper, then break apart some of the tomatoes and chickpeas with a potato masher or fork. Bring the sauce to a simmer.

    13.5g (2 ¼ tsp) salt · 5.4g (2 ¼ tsp) black pepper

  12. Add the pasta directly to the sauté pan and mix with the sauce. Cook the pasta in the sauce according to package directions, about 8 to 12 minutes.

    510g (5 ⅛ cup) penne pasta

  13. To serve, scoop the pasta into a bowl. Enjoy!
