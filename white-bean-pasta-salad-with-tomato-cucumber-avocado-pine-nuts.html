White Bean Pasta Salad with Tomato, Cucumber, Avocado & Pine Nuts

Serves 6 · Cook 40 min

Ingredients

  • 225g (1 ½) avocados
  • 3 cans cannellini (white kidney) beans
  • 1 ½ English cucumbers
  • 540g (1 ½ pints) grape tomatoes
  • 87g (1 ½) lemons
  • 79.8ml (⅓ cup) pine nuts
  • 425g (4 ¼ cup) rotini pasta
  • 80g (2) shallots
  • 1.1g (1 ½ tsp) basil, dried
  • 1.2g (½ tsp) black pepper
  • 79.8ml (⅓ cup) mayonnaise
  • 18g (1 tbsp) salt, divided

Instructions

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with hot water (from the tap), cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes. Drain pasta and rinse under cold water to cool; set aside.

    9g (1 ½ tsp) salt · 425g (4 ¼ cup) rotini pasta

  2. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.

    87g (1 ½) lemons · 540g (1 ½ pints) grape tomatoes · 1 ½ English cucumbers · 225g (1 ½) avocados

  3. Juice lemons into a large bowl.
  4. Peel and mince shallots; add to the bowl with the lemon juice.

    80g (2) shallots

  5. Add mayo and spices to the bowl; whisk to combine the dressing.

    79.8ml (⅓ cup) mayonnaise · 1.1g (1 ½ tsp) basil, dried · 9g (1 ½ tsp) salt · 1.2g (½ tsp) black pepper

  6. Quarter tomatoes and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  7. Trim and small dice cucumbers; add to the bowl.
  8. Halve and pit avocados; scoop out with a spoon and medium dice the flesh. Add to the bowl.
  9. Drain and rinse beans, then add to the bowl.

    3 cans cannellini (white kidney) beans

  10. Add cooked pasta and pine nuts to the bowl, then stir to combine the pasta salad.

    79.8ml (⅓ cup) pine nuts

  11. To serve, divide pasta salad between plates or bowls. Enjoy!
