Black Angus rib-eyes with warm kale, romanesco, and goat cheese salad

Serves 3

Ingredients

  • 1 head organic romanesco
  • 2 ounces organic shredded kale
  • Steak options:
  • 2 Black Angus filet mignons (about 5 ounces each)
  • granulated garlic
  • 3 ounces organic grape or cherry tomatoes
  • 4 or 5 sprigs organic fresh Thai basil
  • 4 or 5 sprigs organic fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • 3 tablespoons crumbled goat cheese
  • porcini powder
  • sweet paprika
  • Lemon vinagrette

Instructions

  1. Prep and cook the romanesco and kale
  2. Prep and cook the steaks
  3. Assemble the kale and romanesco salad
  4. Serve
