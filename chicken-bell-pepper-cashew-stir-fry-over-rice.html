Chicken, Bell Pepper & Cashew Stir-Fry over Rice

Serves 6 · Cook 40 min

Ingredients

  • 277.5g (1 ½ cups) basmati rice
  • 102.8g (¾ cup) cashews, roasted unsalted
  • 1.4kg (3 lb) chicken thighs, boneless skinless
  • 18g (6 cloves) garlic
  • 3 pieces ginger root
  • 714g (6) green bell peppers
  • 1 ½ small bunches green onions (scallions)
  • 20.6g (1 ½ tbsp) brown sugar
  • 16g (2 tbsp) cornstarch
  • 22ml (1 ½ tbsp) rice vinegar
  • 177ml (¾ cup) soy sauce
  • 59ml (4 tbsp) toasted sesame oil, divided
  • 710ml (3 cup) water

Instructions

  1. Using a strainer or colander, rinse the rice under cold, running water, then drain and transfer to a medium saucepan. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil over high heat.

    277.5g (1 ½ cups) basmati rice · 710ml (3 cup) water

  2. Once the liquid comes to a boil, stir the mixture, cover the saucepan, and reduce the heat to low. Cook the rice until the liquid is fully absorbed, 15-18 minutes. Once done, remove the rice from the heat and let it stand, still covered, for 5 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, wash and dry the fresh produce.

    3 pieces ginger root · 714g (6) green bell peppers · 1 ½ small bunches green onions (scallions)

  4. Peel and mince ginger. Peel and mince garlic.

    18g (6 cloves) garlic

  5. Place soy sauce, oil, cornstarch, vinegar, and sugar in a small bowl; whisk to combine the sauce.

    177ml (¾ cup) soy sauce · 44ml (3 tbsp) toasted sesame oil · 16g (2 tbsp) cornstarch · 22ml (1 ½ tbsp) rice vinegar · 20.6g (1 ½ tbsp) brown sugar

  6. Seed and medium dice bell peppers.
  7. Cut chicken thighs into ½-inch pieces.

    1.4kg (3 lb) chicken thighs, boneless skinless

  8. Heat a large pot over high heat.
  9. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom.

    15ml (1 tbsp) toasted sesame oil

  10. Add chicken and stir-fry for 4-5 minutes. Add ginger, garlic, and bell peppers; continue to cook, stirring often, until veggies begin to soften, 4-5 more minutes.
  11. Pour sauce into the pot. Reduce heat to medium-high and cook, stirring often, until the sauce thickens, 6-7 minutes.
  12. Meanwhile, trim green onions on a clean cutting board and cut crosswise into ¼-inch pieces.
  13. Add cashews to the pot and stir to combine.

    102.8g (¾ cup) cashews, roasted unsalted

  14. To serve, divide rice between bowls; top with chicken stir-fry and green onions. Enjoy!
